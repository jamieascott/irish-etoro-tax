Work out your annual Capital Gains from your etoro statements

Upload your etoro annual account statements and get a clear, year-by-year view of what you may owe under Irish tax rules: capital gains, losses to carry forward, dividends and ETF exit tax.

Upload your statements → How it works

This is a demo. Your files are read in your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

What it lets you do

📈

Capital Gains Tax

Charges every disposal to CGT at 33 percent, applying the €1,270 annual exemption, the four-week wash-sale restriction, and relief for current-year and forward losses.

📥

etoro statement import

Reconciles transaction histories from etoro into a single position book, with manual entry for any broker not yet supported.

🔁

Losses carried forward

Upload several years together and see how unused losses carry forward from one year to the next.

⏱️

Four-week rule flags

Highlights losses where the same shares were bought back within four weeks, since those losses may be restricted.

💶

Dividends & withholding tax

Summarises dividends received, the foreign tax withheld, and what may be creditable against Irish tax.

🧺

ETFs & exit tax

Flags EU-domiciled ETFs that fall under exit tax and the 8-year deemed disposal rules, rather than CGT.

🧾

Transaction audit

Lists every disposal and dividend so you can check the figures against your statements.

How it works

  1. Download your etoro account statements On etoro's Account Statement page, choose 1 January to 31 December for each tax year and download the XLSX file.
  2. Upload one or more years Adding earlier years too gives better loss carry-forward figures and a fuller purchase history.
  3. Review the results Work through the summary, CGT detail, dividends and review flags, then take the figures to your return or your accountant.
🔒

Your data stays on your device

Statements and manual entries are processed entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, stored, or shared, and closing the tab clears it. Export your manual entries to CSV to keep them. ECB exchange rates are bundled with the page. Newer rates are only downloaded from the ECB if your dates need them, and that request contains no personal data.

⚠️ Demo only. Not tax advice.

irish-etoro-tax is a demo site that helps Irish retail investors make a first estimate of their annual Capital Gains from etoro statements. The results are estimates to help you prepare. They are not a tax return and not financial or tax advice.

  • Check every figure against your own records and Revenue guidance.
  • Tax rules, rates and exemptions change. The tool may not reflect the latest position or your personal circumstances.
  • Some things it can't fully handle: corporate actions, transfers between brokers, missing history before your first uploaded year, FX methods, and treaty treatment of foreign dividends.
  • Always consult a qualified accountant or tax adviser for complex situations, or before you file.
